mtdrop - AirDrop-compatible file sharing for Windows laptops with a MediaTek MT7921 Wi-Fi card

This is an ALPHA version, tested on one laptop. Please read before installing:

* Only for the MediaTek MT7921 Wi-Fi card (PCI 14C3:7961). The setup checks this and stops otherwise.

* While you receive or send (at most about 2 minutes), mtdrop takes over the Wi-Fi card:
  there is NO Wi-Fi internet during that time. Afterwards the card goes back to the MediaTek
  driver by itself, also after a crash or a restart. Ethernet and USB tethering keep working.

* The setup:
  - copies mtdrop to C:\Program Files\mtdrop and registers the Windows service "mtdrop";
  - downloads the MT7961 Wi-Fi firmware from the openwrt/mt76 project (needs internet now)
    and checks its SHA-256 (the Windows firmware does not accept the commands mtdrop needs);
  - saves a backup of your MediaTek Wi-Fi driver to C:\ProgramData\mtdrop;
  - signs the small mtdrop radio driver with a certificate created on this PC and trusted only
    on this PC. Its private key is deleted right away, so nothing else can be signed with it.
  It does not change the Wi-Fi card itself until you click Receive or Send.

* On the iPhone: AirDrop must be set to "Everyone for 10 Minutes" ("Contacts Only" cannot work).
* To send to an iPhone, Bluetooth must be on.

Uninstall from Settings > Apps removes everything, including the certificate.
